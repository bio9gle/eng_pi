However, this hasty experiment has cast a gloomy shadow over the vibrant social life of our school. Most teenagers remained glued to glowing screens, losing meaningful face-to-face peer conversations throughout the week. Teachers held three emergency counseling sessions regarding classroom focus during afternoon study periods. Anonymous surveys suggest that over seventy percent of faculty members secretly worry about academic decline. Unregulated liberty is nothing more than dangerous negligence, demanding immediate reevaluation by school authorities.
